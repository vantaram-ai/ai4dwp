# Device Setup Stuck During Company Sign-In (Self-Service Guide)

## What this means

If your new or reset work device stops during setup and will not complete sign-in, your files and company data are safe. This issue does not delete your data.

## What you can do now

1. Stay on a stable internet connection.
2. Restart the device once.
3. Try setup again with your work account.
4. If it fails again, wait 10-15 minutes and try one more time.
5. If it still fails, stop and contact the Service Desk.

## Important

Please do not keep retrying many times in a row. Repeated retries usually do not fix this issue and can delay support.

## What to tell the Service Desk

Share these details so support can fix it faster:

- Your full name
- Your work email address
- Device name (if shown on screen)
- Device serial number (if available on sticker/box)
- The exact error message on screen
- Any error code shown (for example: 0x80180014)
- The time the error happened
- A photo of the screen, if possible
- Whether this is a new device or a reset device
- A phone number to contact you

## Reassurance

Your account and data are safe. This is a setup registration issue, and the support team can correct it.

## Contact the Service Desk if...

Contact the Service Desk if setup fails after one restart and one additional retry, or if you see an error code or message that does not clear.
