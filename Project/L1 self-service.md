# Floor 6 self-service

If this happens again on your Floor 6 device, try these steps first:

1. Wait 5 minutes and try signing in again.
2. Check whether your desktop shortcuts are still there after sign-in.
3. If the app opens slowly, close it and reopen it once.
4. Restart your device if sign-in is stuck or very slow.
5. If the issue is still happening, report it to the service desk and include:
   - your name
   - your device name
   - what you saw
   - when it started
   - whether you could sign in

Do not reinstall apps, change permissions, or delete files yourself. This issue may be linked to a recent app rollout, so the service desk needs to check it in a controlled way.

If you were shown a client matter you did not expect to see, report that as a priority right away.
