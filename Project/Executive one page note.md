# Executive one page note

Floor 6 Legal is facing a likely issue linked to a new software change made on Friday. People reported slow sign-ins, trouble getting into their work, and missing desktop shortcuts. One person also said they saw a client matter they were not expecting to see, so we are treating that part with extra care.

## What is being done
We are checking the new software change first, comparing affected and unaffected devices, and reviewing the sign-in and desktop records. We are also looking at the client matter report separately to make sure no one saw information they should not have seen.

## What is still open
We have not confirmed the exact cause yet. We still need to confirm whether the new software change is the main problem, whether the client matter report was a real access issue, and whether the issue is limited to Floor 6 or affecting anyone else.
