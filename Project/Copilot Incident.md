# Copilot Incident

## What it is
This is a potential information-governance and security incident report, not just a product-usability complaint: an end user states Copilot surfaced a client matter they believe they were never authorized to access. At this stage, it is an allegation to confirm, but it carries confidentiality, legal-privilege, and compliance risk until proven otherwise.

## What you would NOT do
Do not close or downgrade this as "AI weirdness" without evidence.
Do not assume Copilot itself created unauthorized access before validating the underlying document permissions and identity context.
Do not ask broad user groups to reproduce the issue in production, which could increase exposure.
Do not alter, purge, or overwrite potentially relevant logs/artifacts before preservation steps are complete.
Do not communicate "false alarm" to leadership until access control truth (ACL/effective permissions) and reproducibility are verified.

## Two-sentence escalation draft
We have a high-priority, to-confirm report that Copilot surfaced client-matter content to a paralegal who states they did not have prior access, so we are treating this as a potential unauthorized information exposure pending validation. We request immediate Security/Compliance and M365 search-permissions support to preserve evidence, verify effective permissions and audit trails for the user/time/content, and provide containment guidance before partner communications.
