## Top 3 Themes to Act On Today (Ranked by Impact and Volume)

1. Account lockouts (Count: 6, Severity: Blocker)
- Why this ranks here: Highest-priority issue because users are fully blocked from access and it affects the largest number of users among blocker themes.
- Manager sentence: "Our top risk today is repeated account lockouts (6 reports) that are fully preventing users from working, so we need immediate containment and identity-side remediation."

2. OneDrive missing files/sync errors (Count: 4, Severity: Blocker)
- Why this ranks here: Also a hard blocker with direct data-access risk; fewer reports than lockouts but high business impact due to missing working files and urgent deadlines.
- Manager sentence: "OneDrive missing-file incidents (4 reports) are a critical continuity risk because staff cannot access required documents for time-sensitive client deliverables."

3. Shared drive access loss (S:/Finance) (Count: 3, Severity: Blocker)
- Why this ranks here: Despite lower volume than some friction themes, this is still a true blocker and directly prevents completion of core finance processes.
- Manager sentence: "Shared drive access failures (3 reports) are blocking finance operations, so restoring S: permissions is a same-day priority over higher-volume but non-blocking issues."

Notes on ranking logic:
- Prioritized Blocker severity over Friction and Minor.
- Used volume as a tie-breaker within the same severity band.
- Applied business-critical context (identity access and file access) when blocker themes were close in count.
