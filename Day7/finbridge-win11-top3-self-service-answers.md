If your account is locked:
1. Wait 15 minutes, then try signing in once with your full work email and password. Do not keep retrying quickly, as this can lock it again.
2. If it still fails, restart your laptop and sign in again before opening other apps.

Contact the Service Desk if you are still locked out after one retry. Share: your username, the exact time it failed, the message shown on screen, and whether this has happened more than once this week.

If OneDrive files are missing:
1. Open OneDrive from the cloud icon near the clock and click “Resume syncing” if paused, then wait 2-3 minutes.
2. Search for the file name in OneDrive on the web (office.com) to check if it is there but not yet synced to your laptop.

Contact the Service Desk if files are still missing after these checks. Share: file names, folder path, when you last saw them, and a screenshot of any OneDrive error message.

If you cannot access the shared S: drive:
1. Disconnect and reconnect VPN, then open File Explorer and try S: again.
2. Sign out of Windows, sign back in, and test the drive before opening many apps.

Contact the Service Desk if S: is still unavailable. Share: your department, exact error message, whether coworkers can access it, and the time of your last failed attempt.
