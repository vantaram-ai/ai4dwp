# Black screen after sign-in

If your screen goes black after you sign in, do not worry. Your work is not lost. It is still safe, and you can try a few simple steps before calling for help.

## What to do

1. Wait for 2 minutes.
Sometimes the screen appears late and then shows your desktop as usual.

2. If the screen is still black, press the Ctrl, Alt, and Delete keys together.

3. Select Log off.

4. Log back in.

5. If the screen is still black after logging back in, turn your computer off and on again.

6. After it starts again, log back in one more time.

## What to expect

If the problem was short-lived, your screen should show your desktop after one of these steps. Your files and work are still safe. If you were working before this happened, your information should still be there when you get back in.

When you raise the ticket, please include:
- Your name
- The time the problem started
- Whether it happened after you logged in
- What you tried already
- Whether turning it off and on again helped
- Any message you saw on the screen
- The device you were using
- Whether this is happening every time or only once

Contact the service desk if the screen stays black after you try these steps, if the problem comes back again, or if you cannot log in at all.
