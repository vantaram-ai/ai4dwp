## Ticket 1 — Paralegal | "I don't have access to that content"

**Subject: Copilot access to the NDA file**

Hi,

Thanks for getting in touch. We've looked into why Copilot said it couldn't access the NDA in SharePoint.

The short answer is: Copilot can only read files that you personally have permission to open. Because you haven't been given access to that folder yet, Copilot is correctly telling you it can't reach it — it isn't a fault with Copilot.

**What to do next:**

1. Contact the matter owner or document owner and ask them to share the folder with your account.
2. Once they've done that, try asking Copilot again — it should be able to summarise the document for you within a few minutes of access being granted.

If you're unsure who owns the folder, reply to this message and we'll help you find out.

## Ticket 2 — New Associate | Copilot can't find case emails in Outlook

**Subject: Copilot in Outlook — your account is still warming up**

Hi,

Welcome to the firm! We've looked into why Copilot isn't finding your case emails yet.

There are two things happening with a brand-new account:

- Your Copilot licence needs to be active and fully assigned — we're checking that now.
- Microsoft needs time to index your mailbox before Copilot can search it. This usually takes between 24 and 72 hours after your account is first set up.

**What to do next:**

1. Please give it until the end of your second working day and try again.
2. If Copilot still can't find emails after 72 hours, reply to this message and we'll escalate to check your licence assignment.

In the meantime, you can still use Copilot inside an open email — open the email directly, then ask Copilot to summarise or respond to it.

## Ticket 5 — Contract Specialist | Copilot giving vague answers about contract templates

**Subject: Copilot and your contract templates library**

Hi,

Thanks for raising this. We've looked into why Copilot is giving generic answers instead of referencing your actual contract templates.

The most likely reason is that the templates library hasn't been fully indexed by Microsoft yet — this can happen if the library was recently set up, moved, or has grown significantly. Until the indexing catches up, Copilot can't read the document contents and falls back on general knowledge instead.

**What to do next:**

1. **Try opening a template directly** in Word or your browser, then use the Copilot panel inside that document and ask it to summarise or explain a clause. This bypasses the search index and should give you accurate, document-specific answers straight away.
2. For library-wide searches, give it another 24–48 hours and try again — indexing usually completes within that window.
3. If the problem persists after 48 hours, reply to this message. We'll check whether a sensitivity label on the library is preventing Copilot from reading the content.
